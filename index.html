Joel Martinsson

Political Science Researcher

I'm a researcher and lecturer in the Department of Political Science at Linnaeus University, Sweden. My work explores the intersection of ethics and democratic governance, asking both what we ought to do and what we are actually doing in political practice.

About

I received my Ph.D. in Political Science from Linnaeus University with a dissertation titled "When Political Ethics Meets Political Practice". My academic journey includes an M.A. in Peace & Conflict from Uppsala University, and dual B.A. degrees in Political Science and Peace & Development from Linnaeus University.

I have had the privilege of serving as a Fulbright Scholar at Harvard University and as a visiting research fellow and committee officer at the Swedish Parliament.

My research tends to combine normative questions (what ought we do?) with empirical questions (what are we doing?) to inform political, democratic, and social practices. I'm particularly interested in how ethical principles manifest—or fail to manifest—in real-world political institutions.

Research

My research interests span three interconnected areas:

Political Ethics

Examining the ethical foundations of political decision-making and how moral principles translate into political practice.

Democratic Governance

Studying how democratic institutions function, including transparency, accountability, and the relationship between politicians and interest organizations.

Pedagogic Innovation

Developing new approaches to teaching political science and ethics in higher education.
