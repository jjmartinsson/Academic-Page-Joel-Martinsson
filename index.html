About Me

I received my Ph.D. in Political Science from Linnaeus University with a dissertation titled "When Political Ethics Meets Political Practice: Dilemmas, Virtues & Vices in the Swedish Parliament". My academic journey includes an M.A. in Peace & Conflict from Uppsala University, and dual B.A. degrees in Political Science and Peace & Development from Linnaeus University.

I have had the privilege of serving as a Fulbright Scholar at Harvard University and as a visiting research fellow and committee officer at the Swedish Parliament.

My research combines normative questions (what ought we do?) with empirical questions (what are we doing?) to inform political, democratic, and social practices. I'm particularly interested in how ethical principles manifest—or fail to manifest—in real-world political institutions.

Focus Areas

Research Interests

Political Ethics

Examining the ethical foundations of political decision-making and how moral principles translate into political practice, including transparency, honesty, and compromise.

Democratic Governance

Studying how democratic institutions function, including accountability, the relationship between politicians and interest organizations, and institutional corruption.

Pedagogic Innovation

Developing engaging approaches to teaching political science and ethics in higher education, including narrative-based workshops and interactive methods.

Dissertation

2024

When Political Ethics Meets Political Practice: Dilemmas, Virtues & Vices in the Swedish Parliament

Doctoral Thesis, Linnaeus University Dissertations

Explores how Swedish parliamentarians reason about ethical dilemmas related to transparency, honesty, and compromise. Introduces powerlessness as a central concept for understanding political dilemmas.

Journal Articles

2025

Virtues in Political Practice: Insights From an Interview Study With Swedish Parliamentarians

Politics & Governance, 13(1), 1–19

Examines which virtues politicians consider most important through in-depth interviews with 74 Swedish parliamentarians. Identifies five key virtue themes: entrepreneurial, social, integrity, wisdom, and craftsman.

2025

Political Judgment Above Transparency? Results From a Mixed Method Study About Politicians' Close Cooperation With Interest Organizations

Governance, 38(2)

A mixed method survey experiment with 1,659 Swedish politicians examining how transparency affects politicians' willingness to cooperate with interest organizations.

2024

Dilemmas of Powerlessness: The Ethical Dimensions of Political Action in the Swedish Parliament

British Journal of Political Science, 54(4), 1239–1255

Shows that politicians' ethical dilemmas stem mainly from powerlessness—both relational (party/constituency loyalties) and inherent (constitutional mandates, limited resources).

Journal of Political Science Education, 21(2), 327–339

Joel Martinsson & Emma Ricknell

Introduces "The Tale of Folke Folkesson," a workshop-based narrative framework where students role-play as methods experts, learning qualitative and quantitative techniques through interactive storytelling.
