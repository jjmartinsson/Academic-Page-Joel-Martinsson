About Me

The short version

I'm fascinated by the gap between what we think politicians should do and what they actually do when faced with real dilemmas.

My research sits at the intersection of political ethics and empirical political science. I ask both normative questions (what should politicians do?) and empirical ones (what are they doing?).

For my PhD, I conducted 74 in-depth interviews with Swedish parliamentarians about ethical dilemmas. Turns out, the biggest challenge for most politicians isn't choosing between good and evil—it's dealing with powerlessness.
