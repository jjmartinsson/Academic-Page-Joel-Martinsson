About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in Political Science at Linnaeus University, where I am based at campuses in both Kalmar and Växjö.

I tend to combine normative questions (what ought we do) with empirical questions (what are we doing) to inform political, democratic, and social practices.

Teaching is an important part of my work, and I currently focus on political theory and methods—two areas I care deeply about. I find great value in helping students engage with fundamental questions about politics while also equipping them with the tools to investigate these questions rigorously.

Current Project: Saving of Swedovia

Saving of Swedovia is a research project generously funded by Vetenskapsrådet (the Swedish Research Council) and the Crafoord Foundation.

As Project Leader (PI), I lead a team at Linnaeus University that includes Veronica Strandh and Anders Hellström.

What We Do

We are developing a serious game designed to explore political judgment and decision-making in the context of climate-related disasters. The game simulates crisis scenarios where players must navigate complex political and ethical dilemmas.

To ensure the game is relevant and realistic for professionals working with climate disasters, we are interviewing practitioners from various fields, including:

  • Emergency management coordinators
  • Municipal crisis planners
  • Climate adaptation specialists
  • Public health officials
  • Policy advisors
"Political judgment involves the capacity to assess situations where established rules and precedents do not straightforwardly apply, requiring an ability to weigh competing values, anticipate consequences, and act decisively under conditions of uncertainty."

Research

My research centers on the intersection of democratic theory, political judgment, and crisis governance. I am particularly interested in how democratic societies can make difficult decisions under uncertainty and how we can cultivate the capacities needed for sound political judgment.

  • Democratic Theory
  • Political Judgment
  • Crisis Governance
  • Climate Politics
